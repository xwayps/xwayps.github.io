We are pleased to announce that we have partnered with the International League of Peoples' Struggle (ILPS).

> "The International League of Peoples' Struggle (ILPS) is an anti-imperialist and democratic formation. It promotes, supports, and develops the anti-imperialist and democratic struggles of the peoples of the world against imperialism and all reaction."

As a decolonial anti-imperialist organization, INLO shares the principles and objectives outlined in the ILPS Charter. Through this partnership, we reaffirm our commitment to supporting the struggles of Indigenous peoples and all peoples affected by colonialism, imperialism, and exploitation, in pursuit of a a free, just, and prosperous future.
