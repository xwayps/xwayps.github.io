The Indigenous National Liberation Organization (INLO) is a political organization dedicated advancing Indigenous rights through education, socialist advocacy, and cultural revitalization. The INLO seeks the recognition of a unified Indigenous state while promoting self-reliance and resisting the commodification of Indigenous culture. We firmly oppose tribal capitalism, settler-nationalist movements, and the liberalization of Indigenous institutions, instead working to develop a distinct Indigenous theory rooted in the lived experiences and struggles of Native peoples.
